2006

Turning the corner

Intel reached its peak operational greenhouse gas emissions and began accelerating investments in abatement and efficiency.

This turning point helped shape the long-term climate work that followed, from cleaner manufacturing to renewable electricity.

2020

RISE strategy begins

Intel introduced its RISE strategy and 2030 goals to make sustainability part of how it designs, builds, and delivers technology.

RISE stands for Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, and Enabling. Its goals cover climate, water, waste, and more.

2022

Net-zero commitment

Intel announced a goal to reach net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across its global operations by 2040.

The commitment focuses on Scope 1 and 2 emissions, with progress supported by efficiency, electrification, and renewable energy.

2023

99% renewable electricity

Intel reported that 99% of its electricity worldwide came from renewable sources, reducing the footprint of its operations.

Renewable electricity is one part of a wider plan that also includes conservation, lower-carbon fuels, and supplier collaboration.

2024

Collaboration grows

Intel brought suppliers, government, and industry leaders together at its Sustainability Summit to share practical solutions.

Semiconductor sustainability depends on the whole value chain, so collaboration helps ideas travel beyond one company.

2040

A net-zero horizon

Intel's long-term target is to achieve net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across global operations.

The horizon is still ahead, making measurement, transparent reporting, and continued innovation essential.
